PRIVACY · RELEASE 1

A little more clarity,
including about your data.

Teinté is designed for guest use. You can explore shades without creating an account. Manual depth and undertone selections are sent only to process the active request and are not saved as a profile or history.

Correction reports

You may submit a report without contact information. If you provide an email address, it is used only to respond about that report and is scheduled for deletion no later than 90 days after submission. Report descriptions and status are retained as editorial records; do not include sensitive personal information.

Technical operation

The service does not use advertising pixels, behavioral analytics, or tracking cookies. Necessary admin session cookies are limited to protected editorial tools. Minimal operational logs exclude request bodies and search terms.

Current launch status

This notice describes the implementation in this repository. Before public deployment, the operator must add a verified contact/controller identity and confirm applicable Philippine privacy requirements and host log settings. No placeholder contact information is published.
