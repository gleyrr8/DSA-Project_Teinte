A NOTE ON MATCHING

Clear labels,
simple choices.

Every comparison starts with the information available in the supplied catalogue. Known-shade results retain the source's own description. Profile results compare the depth and undertone values recorded for each shade.

Read our privacy policy ↗
✳HOW TO READ RESULTS
Exact matchThe original source explicitly identifies the comparison as exact
Not exactThe source describes the shade as lighter, darker, or similar
Exact profile matchBoth the recorded depth and undertone match your selections
Nearby profile matchThe recorded depth is adjacent, or a mixed undertone includes your selection

These labels describe catalogue information, not a guaranteed match on your skin. Actual appearance can vary with lighting, formula, application, and individual complexion.
